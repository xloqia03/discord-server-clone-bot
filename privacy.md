# Privacy Policy - Habab Clone Bot

Habab Agency built the Habab Clone Bot as a free utility tool. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Our bot does not collect, store, or share any personal user data, messages, or sensitive information. It only processes Discord structural objects (roles, channels, categories) temporarily in memory during execution of explicit migration commands.

## 2. Data Security
We value your trust in providing data, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. No data is logged or retained post-execution.

## 3. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
